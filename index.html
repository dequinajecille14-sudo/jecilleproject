The Atelier

Flowers, but make it a feeling.

We began with a single obsession: the lily, after dark. Its architecture, its perfume, the way it holds both drama and grace.

Today, JECILLÉ is a small atelier for grand gestures — single-variety bouquets for the purist, layered compositions for the romantic, and considered gifts for the moments that need more than flowers alone. Everything is chosen by hand, tied by hand and finished with a card we write for you.

Our Story

Born from a
midnight garden.

JECILLÉ began as a private ritual: a single lily on the workbench, composed after the city had gone quiet.

We are a small team of florists, bakers and gift-makers who believe a bouquet should feel like a sentence written only for the person receiving it. So we work in small batches, choose blooms by season, and finish every order by hand.

  • 01

    Rarity

    Small-grower and heirloom varieties, chosen for character rather than convenience.

  • 02

    Craft

    Every stem is conditioned, composed and wrapped at our bench. Nothing is pre-made.

  • 03

    Discretion

    Unmarked deliveries, private messages and a quiet hand for the most personal gestures.

“We do not sell flowers. We compose feelings.”

— The JECILLÉ Atelier
